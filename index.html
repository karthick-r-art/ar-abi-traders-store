Frequently Asked Questions

Does A.R. Abi Traders deliver in Cuddalore?

Yes — we deliver the same day across Cuddalore, including Manjakuppam and nearby areas. Delivery is free on orders above ₹500, and a small ₹40 fee applies below that.

What are your store timings?

We're open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 10pm, and Sunday 9am to 2pm.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes — Cash on Delivery is available, along with UPI / GPay / PhonePe.

Where is the shop located?

No.54/41C, Angalamman Koil Street, Manjakuppam, Cuddalore – 607001. Call us anytime at +91 95665 61558.

Can I order in Tamil?

Yes — switch the language toggle to தமிழ் anywhere on the site, or just use the voice search button to search by speaking.
